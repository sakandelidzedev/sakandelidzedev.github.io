SAKO Agent & Walle Agent

SAKO Agent and Walle Agent are private desktop tools made by Giorgi Sakandelidze. They help the owner manage his own YouTube gaming channels: SAKO Gaming and WalleGameplay.

The tools update the title, description, tags, thumbnail, playlists, translations and comments of the owner's own videos, and read the owner's own channel statistics. They are not offered to other users.
