Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Giorgi Sakandelidze ("we", "our", or "us") operates the ColorFlow: Match & Merge Saga mobile application (the "Game"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Game.

1. Information Collection and Use

ColorFlow is an offline-first puzzle game designed for family and casual entertainment. We do not require account registration or personal identity information (such as name, email, address, or phone number) to play.

Third-Party Services & Advertising

The Game uses Google AdMob to display in-game advertisements (banners, interstitials, and rewarded videos). Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers, such as:

  • Device advertising identifiers (e.g., Apple IDFA, Google Advertising ID)
  • Device model, operating system version, and general location data
  • In-game ad performance and interaction metrics

For more information on how Google uses data, please review Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Local Storage

Game progression, high scores, collected coins, and settings are stored locally on your device storage.

3. Children's Privacy

Our Game does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
